## Confirmed Boundary

The deployed captured build rejects spawn-ready Walderp before attempt admission.
`Main.ManualTitan` supplies native `curEnergy` to `CheckExactSwapCapacity`; at least
one intermediate/final selected gear map has lower capacity. The rejection does
not establish which slot is limiting or the final fight capacity numerically.
Positive native AT3000/3000 and the configured three-death limit remain unchanged.

`BeginRouteProcess` repeats a preserving preflight and defaults to a
Gear/Support/Route/NativeSettings claim with `Recall=None`, no resource lanes.
Deleting the first check would neither authorize nor implement a lower-capacity
transition. The native gear actuator independently protects unclaimed resources.

For total T, idle I, allocations A=T-I and intermediate capacity C, native clamp
safety requires max(0,T-C)<=I, equivalently A<=C. Recall does not itself lower T;
native depletion may be delayed, and increasing capacity does not invent refill.
Funding an over-cap pool before its eventual depletion can consume the required
idle buffer. Ordinary current-pool replay deliberately permits observed over-cap
pools, so it is not automatically a safe lower-cap fight policy.

## Focused Recommendation

The read-only architecture review recommends an explicit, opt-in managed-resource
transition for the manual-titan consumer, preserving all other consumers' default
contract. Temporarily controlled-empty affected lanes are the smallest auditable
policy; capacity-bounded concurrent training can be a later optimization.

1. Pure preflight proves the complete actual swap sequence, immutable/basic and
   unmanaged floors, available lane permissions and owned route retirement.
2. Retire the owned predecessor route before withdrawing prerequisites. The
   receipt-less/UnknownOutcome route from the separate preset failure is NOT owned
   merely because a new claim exists.
3. Declare affected authorized Energy/Magic lanes in the same coupled claim and
   establish their controlled-empty desired maps through the common executor.
4. Apply selected gear/support using an explicit matching recall/depletion
   footprint and exact native observations. A gear no-op cannot skip resource
   policy proof. Basic training/unmanaged/yielded lanes/R3 remain protected.
5. Verify the full coupled grant and actual map/setup at every callback before
   admitting combat. Setup ownership alone is not proof of resource authority.
6. Keep the authorized lanes controlled-empty throughout the bounded attempt so
   ordinary replay cannot consume a delayed-clamp idle buffer.
7. Retire the fight route before release, then reconcile CURRENT B from current
   observations, never saved allocations or original gear. Handle a lower-cap or
   intermediate-dip successor safely. With no goal, preserve unclaimed setup.

Persist the semantic transition footprint/policy, not native references,
allocations, grants or cleanup snapshots. Old persisted records default to their
existing preserving semantics, not fresh recall rights. Failed admission/retry or
rebind cannot renew spawn identity, deaths, original start, owned time or deadline.
